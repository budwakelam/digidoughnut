Good to know

What do I need to run it?

A modern browser on any phone, tablet, or computer. The AI helper uses a free key from Google — Intake walks you through getting one in about two minutes.

Is my food data private?

Yes. Everything is stored on your own device. An optional sync feature can keep a backup copy in the cloud if you want it — your call.

Do I need the AI helper?

No — Intake works fine as a plain food log. The helper just makes logging faster and answers questions when you want it.

Is this medical advice?

No. Intake shows where its numbers come from, but it's a logbook, not a clinician. Talk to a professional about health decisions.
