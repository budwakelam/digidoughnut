📺 TV & phone remote

📺Play it from a Fire TV Stick or Chromecast

Open this address in the stick's web browser:

  1. Line everything up here first, with the projector window in full screen. Then plug the stick into the projector in the same spot.
  2. The address holds your whole setup, so it changes when you change anything. Copy it again after changes.
  3. Connect a phone remote below and the address gets much shorter — and changes you make here show up on the TV by themselves.
  4. On the TV, press OK to start (full screen and sound). Press OK again any time for a jump scare. The right arrow skips to the next scene.
  5. The TV remembers the last setup. Next time, just open the address up to /display/.
  6. Typing tip: the free Fire TV app on your phone has a keyboard for the TV's browser.
  7. Fire TV: turn off the screensaver, and use Display → Calibrate Display so the picture reaches the edges.
  8. Costume photos and your own sound files stay on this computer. They don't go to the TV.

📱Phone remote

Fire jump scares from your phone, and have the TV update by itself when you change things here. Connect your own free Google Firebase database — it's yours, in your own Google account. DigiDoughnut never sees it.

How to set it up (about 10 minutes, once)
  1. Make a free project. Open Firebase, sign in with Google and make a new project. Name it anything. You can switch off Gemini and Google Analytics. It says Spark plan: that's the free one.
  2. Let the app sign in. On the left, click Authentication (tap Get started the first time) → Sign-in method → Anonymous → switch on Enable → Save.
  3. Make the database. On the left, open Realtime Database (not Firestore) → Create Database. Pick United States (Canada/US) or Belgium (UK/Europe) → Start in locked mode → Enable. If it offers App Check, close it.
