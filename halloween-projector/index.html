How it works
  • Line everything up here first, with the projector window in full screen. Then plug the stick into the projector in the same spot.
  • The address holds your whole setup, so it changes when you change anything. Copy it again after changes.
  • Typing tip: the free Fire TV app on your phone has a keyboard for the TV's browser.
  • The TV remembers the last setup. Next time, just open the address up to /display/.
  • On the TV, press OK on the remote to start (full screen and sound). Press OK again any time for a jump scare.
  • Costume photos and your own sound files stay on this computer. They don't go to the TV.
  • Fire TV: turn off the screensaver, and use Display → Calibrate Display so the picture reaches the edges.
⇋ Mirrored on the projector

Drag the orange corners until each shape sits exactly on your real surface. Corners snap to each other (hold Alt to stop snapping). Drag inside a shape to move it. Arrow keys nudge the white corner (Shift = bigger steps). Press B for a jump scare.

Setup

Take a photo of the grid on your house with your phone. Then drag each corner here onto the same numbered square as in your photo. The little marks show the middle of each side.
