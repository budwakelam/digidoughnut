How it works
  • Line everything up here first, with the projector window in full screen. Then plug the stick into the projector in the same spot.
  • The TV stays linked to this page. While this page is open, any change you make here shows up on the TV by itself — no need to copy the address again.
  • Typing tip: the free Fire TV app on your phone has a keyboard for the TV's browser.
  • The TV remembers the last setup and the link. Next time, just open the address up to /display/.
  • Want to scare people from your phone? See Phone remote in the side panel.
  • On the TV, press OK on the remote to start (full screen and sound). Press OK again any time for a jump scare.
  • Costume photos and your own sound files stay on this computer. They don't go to the TV.
  • Fire TV: turn off the screensaver, and use Display → Calibrate Display so the picture reaches the edges.
